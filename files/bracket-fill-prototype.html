RULES

64 movies, 4 brackets, single elimination — same format as an NCAA-style bracket.

Scoring

Each movie's score is its opening weekend (domestic), from Box Office Mojo. Once a movie has a score it keeps that number for the rest of the tournament.

When both movies in a matchup have opened, the higher score advances. Movies release all year, so some matchups won't resolve until December — but a chain reaction can resolve several rounds at once.

Points

Round 1: 1 pt · Round 2: 2 pts · Round 3: 4 pts · Quarter-Finals: 8 pts · Semi-Finals: 16 pts · Champion: 32 pts

Wrong picks don't end your bracket

As long as the movie you picked to advance is the winner of that specific matchup, you score for it.

When movies don't release

If a movie pushes into next year, is changed from a theatrical release to a streaming release or is outright cancelled it scores $0.

Ties

If both movies tie, nobody scores that round and neither advances — their next opponent gets a bye.
