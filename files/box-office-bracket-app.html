RULES

64 movies, 4 brackets, single elimination.

Scoring

A movie's score is its real opening weekend (domestic). Once it has a score it keeps it all year. When both movies in a matchup have opened, the higher score advances.

Points

Round 1: 1 pt · Round 2: 2 · Round 3: 4 · Quarter-Finals: 8 · Semi-Finals: 16 · Champion: 32. Every round is worth 32 in total, so a perfect bracket is 192.

Wrong picks don't end your bracket

If the movie you picked is the real winner of a later matchup, you score for it — even if you got the round before it wrong.

When a movie scores $0

Pushed to next year, cancelled outright, or released straight to streaming.

Ties

The only way to tie is for both films to score $0. Nobody scores and neither advances — an empty slot then counts as a $0 competitor, so whoever it meets next has to post a score above zero to get through.
