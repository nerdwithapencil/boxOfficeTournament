RULES

64 movies, 4 brackets, single elimination.

Scoring

A movie's score is its opening weekend (domestic). Once it has a score it keeps it all year. When both movies in a matchup have opened, the higher score advances.

Points

Round 1: 1 pt · Round 2: 2 pts · Round 3: 4 pts · Quarter-Finals: 8 pts · Semi-Finals: 16 pts · Champion: 32 pts. Every round is worth 32 points in total, so a perfect bracket is 192.

Wrong picks don't end your bracket

If the movie you picked to advance is the winner of a later matchup, you score for it — even if you got the round before it wrong.

When movies don't release

If a movie pushes into next year, is changed from a theatrical release to a streaming release or is outright cancelled it scores $0.

Ties

The only way to tie is for both films to score $0. Nobody scores and neither advances — an empty slot then counts as a $0 competitor, so whoever it meets next has to post a score above zero to get through. If you picked either film it counts as wrong.
